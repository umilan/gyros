GYROSCOPE

This app uses your device's gyroscope for head tracking. Tap the button below to grant access.

OVERVIEW

GYROS renders content twice — once per eye — for a phone-in-headset viewer. Turn your head to look around.

CONTENT

DEMO — rotating wireframe solid. Also on the LOAD menu, to switch back after viewing a model, photo or video.

3D MODEL — local .obj (max ~50 MB) or .stl (max ~5 MB) file. Also accepts .usdz (max ~50 MB), though only the plain-text USDA form is supported — most real USDZ exports use the binary "crate" form and won't load. Decimate high-poly meshes before loading.

VIDEO — local file or streaming URL. Files named with "SBS" auto-enable side-by-side stereo mode.

FOLDER — pick several photos and videos at once (or a whole folder, where supported). You're taken straight to the GALLERY row in the settings list, where −/+ (or the settings strip's ‹/›) steps through them by name. Pick DEMO from LOAD to go back to the 3D demo shapes.

SETTINGS

CONTENT ZOOM — zooms into the content itself (photo, video, model or demo). Pan with POS X/Y to explore once zoomed in.

POSITIONING / ZOOM (XY) — shift and resize the on-screen picture box, and drag its 4 corners (TRAPEZOID), to align it with your lenses. This does not change the content's framing, only where/how big it sits on screen.

BRIGHTNESS — darkens or brightens the content; the settings list and menus are unaffected.

GYRO — multiply, freeze or invert each axis of head tracking. RECENTER sets the current heading as forward.

CONTROLS

GESTURES — drag to pan (POS X/Y), pinch to content-zoom, two fingers up/down adjusts eye distance, tap to show or hide the controls, double-tap to recenter.

SCROLL / PINCH — mouse wheel or trackpad pinch zooms into the content (CONTENT ZOOM), not the on-screen picture box.

KEYBOARD — there's no gear icon: the settings strip is always what appears when the controls are shown, so ↑/↓/←/→ go straight to navigating it (see SETTINGS STRIP below). Tab or clicking/tapping the GYROS logo opens the full list instead. Shift + arrows shifts the image position (X/Y). R resets all settings to defaults. With a video loaded: Space pauses or resumes it, Shift + Space plays it backwards.

SETTINGS STRIP — ↑/↓ move between items and wrap around at either end; ←/→ changes the value; tap the image, or press Esc, to hide the controls. Tab, or the GYROS logo, opens the full list.

FULL LIST — ↑/↓ move between items (past either end closes); ←/→ changes the value, or submits a highlighted row like RESET or CLOSE. WASD works too. Tab or Esc returns to the settings strip.
