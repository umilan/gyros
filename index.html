OVERVIEW

GYROS renders content twice — once per eye — for a phone-in-headset viewer. Turn your head to look around.

CONTENT

DEMO — rotating wireframe solid.

3D MODEL — local .obj or .stl file, max ~5 MB. Decimate high-poly meshes before loading.

VIDEO — local file or streaming URL. Files named with "SBS" auto-enable side-by-side stereo mode.

FOLDER — pick several photos and videos at once (or a whole folder, where supported). You're taken straight to the GALLERY row in the settings list, where −/+ (or the settings strip's ‹/›) steps through them by name. Reload the app to go back to the 3D demo shapes.

SETTINGS

POSITIONING / ZOOM — shift, scale and keystone (TRPZD) each eye image to align it with your lenses.

GYRO — multiply, freeze or invert each axis of head tracking. RECENTER sets the current heading as forward.

CONTROLS

GESTURES — drag to pan, pinch to zoom, two fingers up/down adjusts eye distance, tap to show or hide the controls, double-tap to recenter.

SCROLL / PINCH — mouse wheel or trackpad pinch zooms in and out.

KEYBOARD — while viewing: ↑ opens the settings strip, ↓ opens the full list, ←/→ change eye distance, Shift + arrows shifts the image position (X/Y).

SETTINGS STRIP — ↑/↓ move between items and wrap around at either end; tap the image, or press Esc, to close. ←/→ changes the value.

FULL LIST — ↑/↓ move between items (past either end closes); ←/→ changes the value, or submits a highlighted row like RESET or CLOSE. WASD works too. Esc closes either.
